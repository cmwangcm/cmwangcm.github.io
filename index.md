## About Me

I am a PhD student working on intelligent transportation systems, with a focus on human-driven vehicle behavior modeling, mixed traffic simulation, autonomous vehicle control, and scenario generation.

My current research aims to develop advanced traffic behavior models and simulation frameworks for understanding and improving future mixed traffic systems involving human-driven vehicles, autonomous vehicles, and connected automated vehicles.

## Research Interests

- Car-following and lane-changing behavior modeling
- Mixed traffic simulation
- Knowledge-informed deep learning
- Graph neural networks for vehicle interaction modeling
- Autonomous vehicle control
- OpenDRIVE and CARLA-based scenario generation
