## About Me

Chengming Wang (王诚明) is a PhD student in Computer Science at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU), specializing in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), supervised by <a href="https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/DongyaoJia/">Dongyao Jia</a>, <a href="https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/WeiWang03/">Wei Wang</a>, <a href="https://mengfn.github.io/">Meng Fang</a>, and <a href="https://www.monash.edu/engineering/dongngoduy">Dong Ngoduy</a>.

He obtained his B.Sc. in Mathematics and Statistics from Nanjing University in 2017, followed by an M.Sc. in Big Data Technology from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) in 2018.

Prior to commencing his PhD in 2024, he worked on large-scale AI-driven systems at JD.com, Meituan, and Kuaishou, where he led collaborative teams in designing and deploying AI solutions for complex real-world problems, with applications in supply chain optimization and online advertising systems.

## Research Interests

- AI for Intelligent Transportation Systems
- Modeling and Analysis of Driver Behavior
- Mixed Traffic Modeling and Simulation
- Connected and Autonomous Technologies
